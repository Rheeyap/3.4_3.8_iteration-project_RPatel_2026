About

Welcome to the Tawa College Hockey website , designed to help players of all levels develop their skills and confidence on the field. Whether you are a beginner learning the basics or a more experienced player looking to improve, the site offers clear and helpful guidance on key techniques. You can explore tutorials on skills such as drag flicks, reverse shots (tomahawk), and flicking the ball with better accuracy and control. Each section is made to be easy to understand, so you can practise at your own pace and steadily build your ability.

In addition to skill development, the website also gives you the opportunity to take your training further by connecting with a player from Tawa College. If you would like personalised coaching or one-on-one support, you can simply use the contact form available on the site. This makes it easy to ask questions, organise lessons, and get advice tailored to your needs.

Overall, the website is a great resource for anyone passionate about field hockey and eager to improve their performance while learning from someone with real playing experience.
